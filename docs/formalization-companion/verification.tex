\paragraph{Saved local validation.}
The October 18, 2026 acceptance record covers all 2,771 local modules,
a successful ordinary \texttt{lake build} with 5,717 jobs,
and axiom audits of 40,022 owned declarations, 71 registered results and eight main roots.
Only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} were admitted.
The record includes A/B/C/D comparison cases and controls rejecting weakened statements,
added axioms and a changed definition of left properness.
Initial and separately restored owner-audit logs are byte-identical.
These counts include definitions and helpers, not independent mathematical theorems.
The ARM64 Linux run resumed from a partial cache and combined the main controller with a separately checked restored supplement;
it was not a cold rebuild or a fresh integrated full-controller run.
The unchanged evidence and hash records are in
\href{\Repo/tree/\CatalogueTag/verification/local-linux-20261018}{\texttt{verification/local-linux-20261018/}}.

\paragraph{Correspondence and reuse.}
Comparator checks agreement with fixed Lean statements;
it does not establish their correspondence with the paper.
The main-result Challenges import project definitions rather than specifying every definition independently from Mathlib.
For example, A's 48-line Challenge has a transitive closure of 366 modules and 679,416 lines.
AI-agent review is not independent human review.
Parts of the general Smith machinery use restored closed proof terms;
the catalogue records concrete applications with their stated hypotheses.
Mathlib's general category-theoretic, simplicial and model-category APIs and
Jo\"el Riou's
\href{https://github.com/joelriou/topcat-model-category/tree/6c0c356fea469689fe76baeb47ba773460dfacee}{\texttt{TopCatModelCategory}}
are credited as reused infrastructure in the linked index.
For a paper reference, use the fixed catalogue release for this PDF and
\texttt{v0.2.0-rc1} for the previous formalization release.
